excerpt: "Precise segmentation of skin cancer lesions plays a vital role in computer-aided diagnostic systems. In this study, we introduce ADTNet, an architecture for skin cancer segmentation that integrates dynamic CNNs with transformers using a U-Net framework. The proposed model introduces attention-driven skip connections and transformer modules in the U-Net bottleneck, along with dynamic CNNs for enhanced feature extraction. ADTNet was evaluated on the ISIC 2018 dataset, achieving a Dice score of 92.4% and IoU of 87.4%. The 4-stage version of ADTNet was found to be the optimal configuration, balancing segmentation accuracy with computational efficiency. Our model exhibited performance on par with state-of-the-art techniques, highlighting its potential for practical implementation in real world clinical scenarios."
